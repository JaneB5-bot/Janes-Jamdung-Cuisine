THE STORY BEHIND THE FOOD

It started with home.

For Jane, food has always been more than something to eat. Growing up in Salone — Sierra Leone, West Africa — food was woven into family, culture, celebration, and everyday life.

The kitchen was a place where recipes carried memories and where a meal could bring everybody closer. Those early experiences helped shape Jane's love for cooking and her appreciation for the power of a good plate of food.

Jane's Jamdung Cuisine is a continuation of that love. It is a space where African roots and Jamaican-inspired flavors can meet, creating food that feels familiar while still giving you something new to love.
