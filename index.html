GET TO KNOW JANE

This isn't just food.

My love for food started long before Jane's Jamdung Cuisine ever had a name.

Growing up in Salone — Sierra Leone, West Africa — food was always so much more than what was sitting on the plate. It was family. It was culture. It was laughter in the kitchen, familiar smells filling the house, and the feeling of knowing that there was always something being prepared with love.

Those memories stayed with me.

As I grew older, I realized that cooking gave me a way to hold onto those memories while creating new ones. Every seasoning, every recipe, and every plate carries a little piece of where I come from.

Jane's Jamdung Cuisine is my way of sharing that feeling with you. Whether you're familiar with African and Jamaican food or you're trying it for the very first time, my hope is that every bite makes you feel something.

With love,
Jane ♡
