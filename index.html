02 / HER STORY

From Salone, with love.

For Jane, food has always been more than something to eat.

Growing up in Salone — Sierra Leone, West Africa — food was woven into family, culture, celebration, and everyday life. The kitchen was a place where recipes carried memories and where a meal could bring everybody a little closer.

Jane's Jamdung Cuisine is a reflection of that love. African roots, Jamaican inspiration, and the comfort of food made with care all come together on one table.

Every plate is meant to feel personal — like somebody made it just for you.

Made with love, from Jane's kitchen.
